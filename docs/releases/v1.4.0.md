## Operational Scope and Limits

The full snapshot and incremental paths have distinct staging contracts.
Snapshot reconciliation requires aligned source, staging, and fact data.

Frozen HIGH boundaries constrain candidate selection; they do not retain
historical source values for snapshot replay.

Analytical joins use stored surrogate keys without an IsCurrent filter.
Distinct order counts are not additive across arbitrary dimensional groups.

Development mutation tests require the documented permissions and stable
source/dimension data. Tests 010 and 011 bypass source business triggers
within preparation and cleanup transactions. Audit records and consumed
source identities remain.

Performance and production hardening remain in M8.
Power BI analytics remains in M7.
